Summary

The Dark Knight follows Batman as he attempts to protect Gotham City from the dangerous criminal mastermind known as the Joker. With the help of Commissioner Gordon and Harvey Dent, Batman fights to keep the city safe while facing difficult moral choices.

Actors

Christian Bale plays Bruce Wayne/Batman, Heath Ledger plays the Joker, Aaron Eckhart plays Harvey Dent, and Gary Oldman plays Commissioner Gordon.
